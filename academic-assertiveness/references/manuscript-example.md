# Example: evidence-calibrated editing

This study does not attempt to discuss neural mechanisms. We examine associations between measures of attention and task performance.

The findings may possibly suggest a relationship between the measures.

A number of participants showed somewhat higher performance, but the available results contain no numerical comparison here.

This non-randomized design cannot identify a causal effect. The measured association should not be described as causal.

The results unequivocally establish the mechanism. This would be an unjustified claim without direct evidence.
